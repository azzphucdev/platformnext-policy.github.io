Delete your Platform Next account

This page explains how to delete your account for Platform Next, the VPN app published by Platform Next. You can delete it yourself in the app or on the web. You do not need to install the app to delete your account.

Effective4 August 2026

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account settings first, or billing continues after the account is gone.

In the app

  1. 1Open Platform Next and sign in.
  2. 2Go to the Account tab.
  3. 3Choose Delete account and confirm.

Takes effect immediately.

On the web

  1. 1Open platformnext.dev/#/utilities
  2. 2Sign in with your account.
  3. 3Choose Delete account and confirm.

Takes effect immediately. No app install needed.

Delete your account on the web

If you cannot sign in

Email azzphucdev@icloud.com from the address registered to the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We ask you to send it from that address so we can be sure the request is yours. We reply within 14 working days.
